RENDER ENVIRONMENT
Set these on the BACKEND/API service:

MPESA_PAYBILL_ACCOUNT=<your Co-op Bank account number>
MPESA_PAYBILL_NAME=NEXORA

Do not commit the real bank account number into source control.
